# How to Validate a SaaS Idea Without Coding

Most founders do not fail because they cannot build.

They fail because they build the wrong thing for the wrong person.

Validation is how you replace guessing with evidence before the product surface gets expensive.

---

## What it means to validate a SaaS idea

Validation is evidence that:

- a specific user has a specific problem often enough
- they already try to solve it today
- they would trade time, money, or attention to remove it

---

## The 4 assumptions you must test first

Every SaaS idea starts with four assumptions:

- who has the problem?
- how painful is it?
- what outcome are you promising?
- where will you reach these people?

---

## A 7-day validation sprint

### Day 1 — write the one-sentence promise
### Day 2 — find 20 real people
### Day 3 — send 10 short messages
### Day 4 — run 3 to 5 calls
### Day 5 — create a fake-door test
### Day 6 — ask for commitment
### Day 7 — decide with thresholds

The point is not to feel encouraged.
The point is to collect real signal.

---

## What to do after validation

If the signal is strong, do not jump straight into production architecture.

Move in order.

Start with [SaaS MVP without coding](/blog/how-to-build-a-saas-mvp-without-coding).

If the scope still feels too broad after that, compress it into [single-feature profitable micro-SaaS](/blog/single-feature-profitable-micro-saas).
