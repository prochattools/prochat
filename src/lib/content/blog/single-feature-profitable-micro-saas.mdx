# How to Turn a Single Feature Into a Profitable Micro SaaS

Big platforms fail.

Single features win.

---

## Step 1 — Define One Pain

Not a market.

A pain.

---

## Step 2 — Validate With Landing Page

Collect emails.

Ask for payment intent.

Do not build yet.

---

## Step 3 — Pre-Sell Access

If nobody pays, don’t build.

---

## Step 4 — Build Only Core Flow

Feature → Payment → Dashboard

Nothing else.

---

## Why This Works

Complexity kills speed.

Speed kills doubt.
