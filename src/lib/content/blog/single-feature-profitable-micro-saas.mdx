# Single-Feature Profitable Micro SaaS

The fastest way to break a micro-SaaS is to stop treating it like a micro-SaaS.

Founders often start with one useful feature, get excited by adjacent possibilities, and accidentally rebuild the same bloated platform they were trying to avoid.

---

## What “single-feature” really means

Single-feature does not mean tiny or trivial.

It means the product revolves around one repeatable job that is valuable enough to pay for.

---

## Why this model works

A single-feature product gives you cleaner positioning, faster feedback, simpler onboarding, easier pricing, and lower infrastructure complexity at the start.
