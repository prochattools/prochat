## Why this matters

ProChat is moving from isolated landing pages into a structured authority system. That means every article, glossary entry, prompt, and guide should help builders move to the next decision without leaving the root domain.

## What changes

- Blog posts capture narrative and positioning.
- Docs capture implementation detail.
- Playbooks capture operator workflows.
- Prompts capture reusable AI execution assets.
- Snippets capture tactical patterns.
- Guides connect strategy to execution.
