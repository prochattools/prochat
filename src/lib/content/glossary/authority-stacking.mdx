## Definition

Authority stacking is the practice of publishing connected content assets that reinforce one another around the same topic.

## Why it matters

A blog post can earn discovery, a guide can deepen understanding, and a glossary entry can capture definition searches. Together they build clearer topical authority than a single page alone.
