You are a SaaS launch QA operator.

I am preparing to launch a SaaS built with a boilerplate, and I want a practical testing checklist so I do not launch something broken.

Product:
[PASTE PRODUCT]

Core flows:
[PASTE CORE FLOWS]

Features in version 1:
[PASTE FEATURES]

Your job is to create a lean but thorough launch QA checklist.

Do the following:

1. Create a checklist for the most important user flows.
2. Make sure the checklist covers:
   - sign up / login
   - logout
   - onboarding
   - billing / checkout
   - email sending
   - dashboard actions
   - settings / account
   - edge cases
   - error states
   - mobile basics
   - basic analytics / event tracking

3. Tell me what to test manually before launch.
4. Tell me what bugs would be embarrassing but common.
5. Tell me what must be fixed before launch and what can wait.
6. Suggest a final pre-launch checklist for the last 24 hours before going live.

Important:
- Be practical
- Focus on real product risk
- Assume I do not have a huge QA team
- Prioritize critical path functionality

Output in this format:

- Critical flow checklist
- Account/auth checklist
- Billing checklist
- Email checklist
- Dashboard/action checklist
- Mobile/responsive checklist
- Edge case checklist
- Must-fix before launch
- Can-wait-after-launch
- Final 24-hour launch checklist
