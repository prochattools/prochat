### AI SaaS tool

**Idea:** Ship an AI assistant that delivers insights or creative output on demand.

**Already included:** auth, billing, dashboards, and database models.

**You build:** the prompt engineering, UI, and integrations that make your assistant stand out.

### Client portal

**Idea:** Give your clients a secure portal for reports, documentation, or files.

**Already included:** login, subscription access, dashboard layout, and shared deployment guidance.

**You build:** the client workflows, notifications, and integrations.

### Internal business dashboard

**Idea:** Surface KPIs, approvals, and alerts for internal teams.

**Already included:** tenant-aware database, starter dashboards, and analytics wiring.

**You build:** the widgets, filters, and workflows tailored to the team.

### Micro-SaaS analytics product

**Idea:** Launch a focused insights tool for a niche audience.

**Already included:** billing, onboarding, docs, and deployment scaffolding.

**You build:** the analytics models, UIs, and go-to-market copy.

### Marketplace MVP

**Idea:** Build a curated marketplace with transactions and subscriptions.

**Already included:** authentication, Stripe billing, webhook handling, and documentation scaffolding.

**You build:** the listings, discovery experiences, and commerce automations.
