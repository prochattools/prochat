SaaSKit is the flagship ProChat boilerplate. It combines ProKit infrastructure with the fuller SaaS launch layer, so founders can move from a clear idea into a production-ready build without stitching the foundation together from scratch.

## Who this is for

SaaSKit is the default path for most non-technical founders, solo builders, and small teams that want the fastest route to a real product with auth, billing, deployment, and the launch surface already structured.

If you want more raw control and less built-in product surface, use [ProKit](/docs/prokit) instead.
