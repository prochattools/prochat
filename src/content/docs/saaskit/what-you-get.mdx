### Everything you need to launch a SaaS

SaaSKit delivers the plumbing so you can focus on the idea.

- **Authentication** — Instant login, signup, and session guards, documented in [auth guidelines](/docs/features/auth).
- **Subscription billing** — Stripe plans, metered usage, and webhook automation are wired for day-one revenue; see the [billing doc](/docs/features/billing).
- **Database models** — Tenant-aware Postgres schemas are configured so you can model data quickly.
- **Admin dashboard** — Starter UI, analytics hooks, and SEO-ready pages are ready for your branding.
- **Landing pages** — Marketing, docs, and lead capture flows ship alongside the app.
- **Deployment setup** — Node 20, Dokploy, and Vercel build templates accelerate shipping; review the [deployment guide](/docs/features/deployment).
- **Email infrastructure** — Shared MailerLite flows keep onboarding and alerts consistent.

Hand this page to stakeholders who want to understand what the boilerplate covers without diving into code.
