ProKit is the lighter ProChat boilerplate. It gives you the core runtime foundation for a SaaS product without the fuller SaaSKit productization and launch layer.

## Who this is for

Choose ProKit if you already know your scope, want more control over the product surface, and do not need the fuller default founder path that SaaSKit provides.

If you want the clearest production-ready route with more built-in launch structure, use [SaaSKit](/docs/saaskit) instead.

## When to choose ProKit instead of SaaSKit

Choose ProKit when:

- you want the underlying SaaS engine but not the fuller SaaSKit layer
- you are comfortable owning more of the product structure yourself
- you already know why the lighter foundation is the better fit

If any of that is still uncertain, default to [SaaSKit](/docs/saaskit).

## How ProKit fits the ProChat path

ProKit is the secondary option in the product hierarchy. It is valuable when you want the engine layer and are willing to own more of the launch structure yourself. Most non-technical founders should still start with [SaaSKit](/kits/saaskit).
