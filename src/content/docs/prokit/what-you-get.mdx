### Everything you need to launch a SaaS

ProKit removes weeks of setup work so you can focus on the experience your customers love.

- **Authentication** — Users can sign up, log in, and manage their accounts from day one. The shared [auth docs](/docs/features/auth) explain how it works.
- **Subscription billing** — Stripe is already wired for plans, trials, and metered events so you can charge customers immediately. Learn more in the [billing reference](/docs/features/billing).
- **Database models** — Tenant-safe PostgreSQL schemas are configured and documented, leaving you free to design the product data you care about.
- **Admin dashboard** — Starter pages, analytics hooks, and SEO-ready landing routes are ready for branding and storytelling.
- **Landing pages** — The marketing shell includes docs, promo, and lead-capture flows so you can launch without rebuilding each page.
- **Deployment setup** — Node 20 builds, Dokploy automation, and published guidance let you deploy in minutes; see the [deployment guide](/docs/features/deployment).
- **Email infrastructure** — MailerLite funnels and shared automation keep onboarding, alerts, and transactional messages consistent.

Use this page whenever someone asks what the kit delivers—each item is already wired so you can ship features fast.
