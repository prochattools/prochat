### AI SaaS tool

**Idea:** Launch a productivity assistant that ships insights, templates, or copilots.

**Already included:** authentication, subscription billing, dashboard pages, tenant-safe database models, and shared deployment guidance.

**You build:** the AI prompt logic, custom UI, and integrations that make the product unique.

### Client portal

**Idea:** Give your customers a secure space with reports, files, and notifications.

**Already included:** secure auth flows, billing, webhook-ready Stripe connection, and a starter dashboard shell.

**You build:** the portal experiences, invoices, and product-specific automations.

### Internal business dashboard

**Idea:** Surface operations KPIs, approvals, and alerts for your team.

**Already included:** user access controls, database scaffolding, dashboards, and analytics wiring.

**You build:** the charts, filters, and workflow automations tailored to your team.

### Micro-SaaS analytics product

**Idea:** Package a focused insights tool for a niche audience.

**Already included:** billing, dashboard, landing pages, and deployment/docs automation.

**You build:** the analytics models, onboarding flows, and domain-specific UI.

### Marketplace MVP

**Idea:** Launch a curated marketplace that needs subscriptions and transactions.

**Already included:** authentication, Stripe billing, webhook wiring, and documentation scaffolding.

**You build:** listings, discovery, order management, and integrations.
