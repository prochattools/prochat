ProKit is designed for:

- Non-technical founders who still want production-ready apps.
- Indie developers shipping their next SaaS MVP.
- Startup teams needing a secure dashboard, billing, and governance.
- Agencies building client portals that must scale with auth/billing.

Use this page to align stakeholders on why the kit exists and what it accelerates.
