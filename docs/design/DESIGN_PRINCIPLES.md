# ProChat Design Principles

**Status:** canonical repository-local principles  
**Authority:** `DESIGN.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, Mind company philosophy

## Purpose

These principles govern every public page, product visualization, component, animation, and design review.

## Principles

### 1. Structure before decoration

Use hierarchy, alignment, spacing, grouping, and explicit relationships before ornament, effects, or illustration.

### 2. Evidence stays visible

When a conclusion, memory, or action depends on evidence, show the relationship. Do not represent trust as unexplained magic.

### 3. Human control is a visible state

Draft, review, approval, rejection, confirmation, correction, and retirement must be distinguishable in copy and visuals.

### 4. Every visual explains

A major visual must communicate a product mechanism, state change, boundary, comparison, or outcome. Decorative visuals cannot carry the page.

### 5. Premium through precision

Quality comes from typography, composition, timing, realistic details, responsive behavior, accessibility, and performance—not visual excess.

### 6. Calm before exciting

The system should feel stable and trustworthy first. Motion and contrast may create energy only when they support comprehension.

### 7. One dominant idea per section

Every section has one primary proposition, one primary visual idea, and one intended visitor understanding.

### 8. Static quality precedes motion

Every cinematic section must work as a static composition before animation is added.

### 9. Motion reveals cause and effect

Motion may show where an object came from, why its state changed, which evidence affected it, or how it is reused. Motion must not exist only to attract attention.

### 10. Mobile is a distinct composition

Do not shrink desktop canvases. Recompose the narrative into readable mobile scenes with normal document scrolling.

### 11. Accessibility is a design input

Keyboard behavior, semantic order, contrast, zoom, touch, reduced motion, and assistive-technology meaning are designed from the start.

### 12. Performance is a design constraint

A visual concept that cannot meet approved performance budgets must be simplified or deferred.

### 13. One global identity

All ProChat products use Golos Text, JetBrains Mono, grayscale, and ProChat Cobalt. Products differ through structure, density, content, and behavior.

### 14. Honest boundaries build trust

Do not imply capabilities, privacy, integrations, company scale, or outcomes that are not currently supported.

### 15. Reuse without generic sameness

Shared primitives should create consistency, but each page must have a clear narrative and product-specific visual composition.

## Review questions

Before approval, ask:

1. What is the one idea?
2. What product mechanism does the visual explain?
3. Is the evidence or state visible?
4. Does the static version work?
5. Does mobile have its own composition?
6. Does reduced motion preserve the conclusion?
7. Is the claim supported?
8. Does the result meet performance and accessibility budgets?
9. Could this belong to any generic AI company? If yes, redesign it.
