## Concept summary

The ProChat homepage should use one continuous cinematic memory sequence as a narrative background layer.

Vertical scrolling does not merely move the page downward. It advances or reverses the sequence frame by frame. Concise text and interface elements appear over the sequence at specific narrative moments to explain what the viewer is seeing.

The film visualizes the outcome and philosophy of **ProChat Memory**, not a literal software interface.

The central story is:

```text
fragmented human experience
→ captured memory units
→ detected relationships
→ structured and reviewed memory
→ durable personal context
→ timely recommendations
→ a trusted memory that grows across a lifetime
```

## Product truth represented

The sequence should communicate that ProChat Memory:

- is the customer-facing expression of the Infinite Brain philosophy;
- preserves durable memory independently of a particular LLM, operating system, device, or vendor;
- stores portable, human-readable memory rather than trapping identity in a hosted platform;
- becomes more useful, personal, and context-aware through continued use;
- connects past evidence, present context, and possible future action;
- helps technology work for the person rather than forcing the person to manage technology;
- remains user-owned, inspectable, correctable, and governed by human decisions.

The visual must not imply that currently unimplemented long-term capabilities already exist.

## Primary visual language

### Memory threads

Fine organic lines represent thoughts, observations, evidence, conversations, decisions, and experiences.

Initially they are:

- dispersed;
- overlapping;
- incomplete;
- differently weighted;
- difficult to interpret.

As the narrative progresses, they become:

- grouped;
- traceable;
- categorized;
- prioritized;
- connected to sources;
- organized into coherent memory threads.

The lines should not resemble generic decorative neural-network graphics. Their behavior must communicate information becoming understandable.

### Memory units

Translucent cards or planes represent discrete memory units.

They may contain restrained abstract traces such as:

- a date;
- a decision marker;
- a source indicator;
- a short text fragment;
- a confidence or relevance signal;
- a relationship point.

Text inside the generated film should be minimal or absent because generative video text is unreliable. Real semantic labels should be rendered as accessible webpage overlays.

### Temporal stream

Memory units travel in an organic stream:

- older memories recede into atmospheric depth;
- current context becomes sharper and more legible;
- possible future directions remain luminous but unresolved;
- paths may loop backward to reveal origins;
- related threads may converge, diverge, or be superseded.

The stream should feel holistic rather than mechanically linear.

### Brain decomposition and integration

A brain-like whole may briefly form from many memory elements, then separate into its constituent evidence, threads, and units.

This is symbolic rather than anatomical. Avoid medical imagery, literal neurons, or a science-fiction hologram cliché.

The meaning is:

```text
one coherent understanding
is composed of many inspectable memories
```

## Narrative arc

### Act 1 — Friction

A person works through an old, rigid computer interface. Inputs are fragmented. Multiple windows, prompts, notes, and traces remain disconnected.

The person should be represented indirectly or minimally. The focus is the friction between human thought and technology, not a character performance.

Meaning:

- the person must repeatedly explain context;
- useful thought disappears into tools;
- technology requires the human to adapt to it.

### Act 2 — Capture

Fragments detach from the old interface and become translucent memory units.

Fine lines begin connecting related pieces, but the system remains partially unstructured.

Meaning:

- useful work begins leaving memory behind;
- varied inputs enter one durable memory layer.

### Act 3 — Structure

The camera moves through the memory field.

Threads become clearer. Evidence connects to decisions. Repeated patterns form. Important paths gain visual weight while irrelevant noise recedes.

Meaning:

- ProChat Memory structures rather than merely stores;
- relationships and provenance make memory useful and trustworthy.

### Act 4 — Continuity

The visual travels backward along a thread to its origin, then returns to the present carrying context forward.

Older memories remain visible but are visually marked as historical, superseded, or less relevant.

Meaning:

- history remains available without becoming current identity;
- present intent outranks outdated patterns;
- memory survives devices, models, and time.

### Act 5 — Assistance

A current situation appears. The memory system quietly assembles only the relevant threads and presents one restrained recommendation or insight.

The visualization must show evidence and confidence without making the decision.

Meaning:

- the right context appears at the right moment;
- ProChat Memory produces conclusions and recommendations;
- the human remains the decision-maker.

### Act 6 — Expansion

The view pulls back to reveal that the organized memory is not a closed database but a living, extensible constellation.

Personal, business, faith, philosophy, work, and learning domains may be suggested through abstract clusters without exposing private content.

The sequence ends with calm coherence rather than spectacle.

Meaning:

- the memory compounds across a lifetime;
- it remains portable and model-agnostic;
- technology increasingly works for the person.

## Scroll experience

The preferred implementation is a pinned or sticky cinematic section whose progress maps to a pre-rendered frame sequence.

```text
vertical scroll progress
→ normalized timeline position
→ selected image frame
→ synchronized text-overlay state
```

The user may scroll forward and backward through the sequence.

The page must not lock or trap scrolling. The cinematic sequence should occupy a bounded section with a clear beginning and end.

## Overlay content principles

Text overlays should:

- remain real HTML, never baked into generated frames;
- be concise;
- explain the meaning of the current visual state;
- fade or move with restraint;
- maintain readable contrast;
- remain fully accessible without the film;
- preserve the approved product hierarchy and claim boundaries.

The film is the background narrative. The overlays carry semantic claims and actions.

## Candidate overlay beats

1. Useful work should leave useful memory behind.
2. Your context should not disappear when a conversation ends.
3. Fragments become reviewed, reusable memory.
4. Every conclusion remains connected to its evidence.
5. History informs you without defining who you must remain.
6. The right context appears when it matters.
7. Your memory remains yours—across models, devices, and time.
8. ProChat Memory becomes more useful through the work you already do.

These are draft execution lines, not approved final copy.

## Visual character

The desired visual character is:

- cinematic;
- calm;
- premium;
- organic;
- precise;
- emotionally resonant;
- dark but not ominous;
- technologically advanced without science-fiction excess;
- human rather than clinical;
- spectacular through depth and continuity rather than constant activity.

## Color direction

Do not finalize color before visual-direction exploration.

Initial candidate behavior:

- deep near-black or midnight background;
- restrained burgundy, violet, or warm magenta depth;
- pale neutral memory nodes;
- subtle warm signals for trusted or current memory;
- cooler, more distant treatment for historical or uncertain context;
- limited color coding with semantic purpose.

Avoid neon rainbow networks, cyberpunk blue, excessive glow, and decorative gradients without meaning.

## Typography

Current starting point:

- Golos Text for primary communication;
- JetBrains Mono for provenance, dates, confidence, source markers, or technical metadata.

A more human editorial or classical typeface may be explored if the visual direction demonstrates a clear improvement. Typography is not considered permanently locked by this brief.

## Product hierarchy

The homepage remains company-led and ProChat Memory-led.

ProChat Workbench may appear later in the narrative as the second product: a way to put local project context to work through ChatGPT. It must not compete with the flagship memory story in the cinematic opening.

## Trust presentation

Trust must be demonstrated through product behavior and founder evidence rather than generic badges.

Potential evidence:

- local and Markdown-first ownership;
- portability across models, computers, and operating systems;
- source traceability;
- review and correction;
- visible distinction between fact, inference, confidence, and decision;
- founder experience in QA and disciplined verification;
- evidence that ProChat uses its own systems in real product work.

Founder credibility must be presented accurately and must not turn the company site into a personal-services portfolio.

## Conversion role

The cinematic sequence is primarily an understanding and emotional-identification mechanism.

It should lead toward the approved primary action:

```text
Explore ProChat Memory for QA
```

The CTA should appear when the viewer understands:

- the problem of disappearing context;
- the durable-memory model;
- the trust model;
- the current concrete QA entry point.

## Technical production plan

### Generation

Create the film separately with a controllable multimodal video model using:

- a master visual brief;
- reference images for lines, memory cards, depth, materials, and camera behavior;
- shot-specific prompts;
- consistent palette and geometry;
- a defined first and final frame for each shot where supported.

Do not rely on one long prompt to generate the complete narrative reliably.

### Duration

The desired narrative may be approximately 20 seconds, but the production workflow should permit multiple coordinated shots that are edited into one seamless master sequence.

### Post-production

- remove or avoid generated text;
- stabilize color and exposure;
- ensure seamless transitions;
- reserve negative space for webpage overlays;
- export a high-quality master;
- create desktop and mobile crops or alternate compositions where required.

### Frame extraction

Extract optimized image sequences from the approved master using FFmpeg.

The final format, frame rate, resolution, and compression require performance prototyping. Do not assume that every source-video frame should be shipped unchanged.

Candidate approach:

- generate at cinematic frame rate;
- extract or resample to a lower effective scroll frame rate;
- encode responsive AVIF or WebP frames;
- preload only the required window around current scroll progress;
- preserve a poster frame and non-JavaScript fallback.

### Web implementation

Potential implementation methods to prototype:

1. responsive image-sequence canvas with semantic HTML overlays;
2. video current-time scrubbing where browser behavior is sufficiently reliable;
3. hybrid frame atlas or segmented sequence;
4. static keyframe progression for reduced-motion and constrained devices.

No method is approved until measured for:

- loading cost;
- memory consumption;
- scroll smoothness;
- mobile thermals;
- visual fidelity;
- accessibility;
- browser consistency.

## Accessibility and restraint

- The story must remain complete without animation.
- Reduced-motion users receive a static or stepped sequence.
- Screen readers receive semantic equivalent content.
- Text and CTAs remain HTML.
- The film cannot block keyboard navigation.
- A user must be able to move past the sequence normally.
- Decorative complexity must not obscure claims or controls.

## Direction decision — human presence and initial duration

### Human presence

The preferred direction combines abstract visualization with subtle human presence.

```yaml
fully_visible_character: NO
abstract_memory_system: PRIMARY
human_presence: INDIRECT
```

The opening frame must immediately communicate memory, intelligence, and continuity rather than beginning with a literal old computer interface.

Human presence may be suggested through:

- a silhouette or reflection;
- a hand or gesture;
- a head or brain-like profile used symbolically;
- the spatial scale and camera perspective of moving through one's own memory;
- subtle transitions from mechanical interface traces into organic memory structures.

The visual must avoid making the homepage look like a medical, neuroscience, robotics, or generic AI company.

### Hero requirement

The first viewport must stand on its own before any scroll interaction.

It should contain:

- a clear ProChat Memory identity;
- a concise hero claim rendered as HTML;
- a visually coherent memory system already visible in its first frame;
- an immediate indication that memory is connected, durable, and user-owned;
- a clear primary action.

The scroll sequence should deepen understanding rather than rescue an unclear hero.

### Reference qualities added

The latest image references support:

- luminous flowing data strands that converge into organized channels;
- repeated modular forms that expand, separate, and recombine;
- a complex whole composed of many small inspectable parts;
- human/technology duality expressed symbolically;
- mechanical structures transforming into organic cognitive structures;
- high-contrast cinematic scenes with restrained text overlays;
- product storytelling where one persistent visual evolves behind changing copy.

Avoid literal half-human/half-machine imagery as a final brand symbol unless later testing proves it does not imply replacement, transhumanism, surveillance, or loss of human agency.

### Recommended master duration

Start with one **12-second master sequence at 24 frames per second**.

```yaml
master_duration_seconds: 12
source_frame_rate: 24
source_frames: 288
recommended_web_frames_initial: 144
recommended_web_sampling: 12_fps_effective
```

Rationale:

- Twelve seconds is long enough for a six-beat transformation without forcing unnecessary filler.
- It remains within Seedance 2.0's officially described maximum 15-second output.
- At 24 fps it provides 288 source frames for editing and retiming.
- The website should initially ship approximately 120–180 optimized frames rather than every source frame.
- Scroll distance, not video duration alone, controls how slowly the viewer experiences the sequence.

Recommended first prototype:

```text
12-second master
→ 288 source frames
→ resample to 144 web frames
→ map to approximately 500–700 viewport-heights of scroll progress after UX testing
```

The final scroll distance must be determined through prototype testing; it should not be inferred directly from video seconds.

### Multiple-clip threshold

Use a single 12–15 second generation if the model can maintain continuity and the storyboard remains one camera journey.

Use two coordinated clips only when:

- the transformation requires a major scene change;
- visual consistency cannot be maintained in one generation;
- a clean edit point can be designed;
- the complete narrative genuinely needs more than 15 seconds.

Do not target 20 seconds by default. Longer duration increases generation inconsistency, page weight, frame count, and scroll fatigue without necessarily improving the story.
