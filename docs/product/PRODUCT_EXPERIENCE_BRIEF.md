## Approved strategic foundation

- ProChat is a memory-first software company.
- ProChat Memory is the flagship product.
- ProChat Memory for QA is the current discipline-specific edition and primary conversion path.
- ProChat Workbench is the second product.
- The durable asset is reviewed memory, not the underlying model or interface.
- ProChat Memory is the customer-facing product expression of the Infinite Brain philosophy.
- Mind owns human meaning, identity, philosophy, strategy, evidence, and history.
- Brain owns machine capabilities, retrieval, automation, enforcement, and runtime.
- ProChat public platform expresses the company and products through marketing and product communication.
- Trust, human agency, faithful representation, user ownership, inspectability, correction, portability, and evidence are foundational.
- Long-term philosophy must not be marketed as current capability without product evidence.

## Reference analysis 1 — 50 Years Swiss Music Charts

**Source:** `https://50-jahre-hitparade.ch/`  
**Reference role:** spatial memory visualization and scroll-driven narrative  
**Copying rule:** extract interaction and storytelling principles only; do not copy the site's visual identity, chart data, geometry, typography, or assets

### Owner-observed qualities

- Scrolling behaves like travelling or zooming into a spatial system rather than moving down a conventional page.
- The experience presents time as a navigable visual field.
- Lines, dots, and labels create a network of relationships.
- The visual can be reinterpreted for ProChat as memory threads, connections, origins, and related decisions.
- Sparse typography allows the visual model to carry the story.
- Credibility comes from showing the underlying information structure rather than merely claiming that structure exists.

### Verified source behavior

The source describes itself as a 3D WebGL experience, instructs users to scroll or swipe to explore, and uses line connections, position, and scale to encode chart progression and meaning. The source also explicitly states that the WebGL experience is not accessible to screen readers.

### ProChat design principles extracted

1. **Spatial depth instead of ordinary scrolling**  
   A scroll gesture may progressively reveal deeper layers of a memory rather than only translating the viewport vertically.

2. **Memory as relationships**  
   Memory should be visualized as connected evidence, decisions, events, people, and outcomes—not as decorative neural-network imagery.

3. **From unstructured to structured**  
   The narrative may begin with dispersed fragments and gradually form explicit threads, clusters, provenance, and approved memory.

4. **Zoom as explanation**  
   Moving deeper should reveal why a memory exists: source evidence, prior decisions, related patterns, confidence, and current relevance.

5. **Visual proof builds trust**  
   The animation should demonstrate how information becomes traceable and reusable rather than merely stating that ProChat remembers.

6. **Restraint**  
   Sparse copy, controlled labels, and a limited number of visible relationships should prevent the experience from becoming a chaotic technology diagram.

### Candidate homepage use

A bounded hero or early narrative sequence could show:

```text
raw fragments
→ detected relationships
→ memory thread
→ reviewed conclusion
→ reusable context
```

A second possible sequence could travel backward through a memory thread to reveal its origin, then forward to show how that memory informs a current recommendation.

### Interaction constraints

- The visualization must support the homepage narrative rather than replacing navigation or content hierarchy.
- Essential claims and actions must remain understandable without animation.
- Scroll hijacking must be avoided; users must retain predictable control.
- Reduced-motion users require a clear static or stepped alternative.
- Keyboard and touch interaction must remain complete.
- The semantic content must remain accessible to screen readers.
- WebGL or canvas must not become the sole carrier of meaning.
- Performance budgets and lower-powered mobile devices must be considered before selecting 3D rendering.

### Emotional attributes supported

- exploratory;
- intelligent;
- interconnected;
- deep;
- calm;
- precise;
- quietly surprising.

### Anti-attributes identified

- chaotic neural-network decoration;
- visual complexity without meaning;
- inaccessible canvas-only storytelling;
- forced scrolling;
- excessive labels;
- novelty motion that competes with the product promise.
