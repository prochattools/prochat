# A2 Review Gate — Motion Lab Visual Identity

**Scope:** isolated HyperFrames proof-of-concept only. This file does not replace repository `DESIGN.md`.

## Style Prompt

Calm technical cinema for a memory-first software product. The composition should feel precise, editorial, and product-grounded: dark graphite surfaces, cool neutral borders, restrained ProChat Cobalt accents, realistic evidence UI, visible provenance, and deliberate depth. Motion should communicate a state transition from unreviewed evidence to reviewed durable memory. The frame must feel like a premium product film, not an AI demo reel.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#0d1118`

- ProChat Cobalt deep: `#3158c7`
- Success: `#56b98e`
- Error: `#d76b64`

## Typography

- Primary: Golos Text; system sans fallback permitted for isolated lab preview.
- Technical metadata: JetBrains Mono; system monospace fallback permitted.
- Large copy uses tight editorial tracking and restrained weight.

## Motion

- One continuous 5.2 second transformation.
- Evidence objects enter with slight depth offsets, never random motion.
- Review Gate is a state boundary, not a decorative laser.
- Approved evidence crosses and resolves into a structured memory stack.
- Rejected evidence remains visible but demoted.
- Camera stays fixed; depth comes from scale, opacity, shadow, and spatial ordering.
- No infinite loops.

## What NOT to Do

- No laser fields or particle clouds.
- No glowing AI blobs or neon cyberpunk.
- No generic floating cards without product semantics.
- No rainbow gradients or colored glow shadows.
- No cartoon icons or anthropomorphic AI imagery.
- No random movement.
- No fake product screenshots.
